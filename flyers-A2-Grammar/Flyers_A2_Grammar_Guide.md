# Cambridge Flyers (A2) 语法知识点整理

**Grammar Points & High-Frequency Verbs**

---

## 一、时态 (Tenses)

### 1.1 一般现在时 (Simple Present)

**用法：** 描述习惯、事实、规律

| 人称 | 肯定句 | 否定句 |
|------|--------|--------|
| I / You / We / They | I play football. | I don't play football. |
| He / She / It | He plays football. | He doesn't play football. |

**高频动词第三人称单数变形：**

| 原形 | 第三人称单数 | 原形 | 第三人称单数 |
|------|-------------|------|-------------|
| go | goes | do | does |
| have | has | watch | watches |
| study | studies | play | plays |
| like | likes | live | lives |
| eat | eats | drink | drinks |

---

### 1.2 现在进行时 (Present Continuous)

**用法：** 描述正在进行的动作  
**结构：** am/is/are + V-ing

| 人称 | 肯定句 | 否定句 |
|------|--------|--------|
| I | I am reading. | I am not reading. |
| He / She / It | She is reading. | She isn't reading. |
| You / We / They | They are reading. | They aren't reading. |

**高频动词 -ing 形式变化规则：**

| 规则 | 原形 | -ing 形式 |
|------|------|----------|
| 直接加 -ing | play, read, eat | playing, reading, eating |
| 去 e 加 -ing | make, write, come | making, writing, coming |
| 双写末尾字母加 -ing | run, swim, sit | running, swimming, sitting |
| ie 变 y 加 -ing | lie, die | lying, dying |

---

### 1.3 一般过去时 (Simple Past)

**用法：** 描述过去发生的动作  
**时间标志词：** yesterday, last week, ago, in 2020

**⭐ 高频不规则动词过去式（必背）：**

| 原形 | 过去式 | 原形 | 过去式 | 原形 | 过去式 |
|------|--------|------|--------|------|--------|
| be | was/were | go | went | have | had |
| do | did | see | saw | come | came |
| get | got | make | made | take | took |
| give | gave | eat | ate | drink | drank |
| run | ran | swim | swam | write | wrote |
| read | read | put | put | cut | cut |
| buy | bought | bring | brought | think | thought |
| teach | taught | catch | caught | find | found |
| tell | told | say | said | know | knew |
| fly | flew | fall | fell | feel | felt |

**规则动词过去式变化规则：**

| 规则 | 例词 | 过去式 |
|------|------|--------|
| 直接加 -ed | play, watch, walk | played, watched, walked |
| 以 e 结尾加 -d | like, love, live | liked, loved, lived |
| 辅音+y 结尾，y 变 i 加 -ed | study, carry, try | studied, carried, tried |
| 双写末尾字母加 -ed | stop, plan, drop | stopped, planned, dropped |

---

### 1.4 一般将来时 (Simple Future)

**用法：** 描述将要发生的动作  
**结构：** will + 动词原形 / be going to + 动词原形

| 结构 | 肯定句 | 否定句 |
|------|--------|--------|
| will | I will go. / I'll go. | I won't go. |
| be going to | I am going to go. | I'm not going to go. |

---

### 1.5 现在完成时 (Present Perfect)

**用法：** 表示过去发生但与现在有关联的动作  
**结构：** have/has + 过去分词  
**常用标志词：** already, yet, just, ever, never, since, for

**高频动词过去分词：**

| 原形 | 过去分词 | 原形 | 过去分词 | 原形 | 过去分词 |
|------|----------|------|----------|------|----------|
| be | been | go | gone/been | have | had |
| do | done | see | seen | eat | eaten |
| write | written | take | taken | give | given |

## 二、情态动词 (Modal Verbs)

**特点：** 后面接动词原形，没有人称和数的变化

| 情态动词 | 用法 | 肯定例句 | 否定例句 |
|----------|------|----------|----------|
| can | 能力、许可 | I can swim. | I can't swim. |
| could | 过去的能力、礼貌请求 | Could you help me? | I couldn't find it. |
| may | 许可、可能性 | May I come in? | You may not leave. |
| might | 较小的可能性 | It might rain. | It might not work. |
| must | 必须、肯定推测 | You must study. | You mustn't run. |
| should | 应该、建议 | You should rest. | You shouldn't eat that. |
| would | 礼貌请求、意愿 | Would you like tea? | I wouldn't do that. |
| have to | 不得不（外部要求） | I have to go now. | You don't have to go. |

## 三、比较级和最高级 (Comparatives & Superlatives)

### 3.1 形容词变化规则

| 规则 | 原级 | 比较级 | 最高级 |
|------|------|--------|--------|
| 单音节：加 -er/-est | tall, old, young | taller, older, younger | tallest, oldest, youngest |
| 以 e 结尾：加 -r/-st | large, nice, late | larger, nicer, later | largest, nicest, latest |
| 辅+元+辅：双写+er/est | big, hot, thin | bigger, hotter, thinner | biggest, hottest, thinnest |
| 以 y 结尾：变 y 为 i | happy, easy, busy | happier, easier, busier | happiest, easiest, busiest |
| 多音节：more/most | beautiful, interesting | more beautiful | most beautiful |

### 3.2 不规则变化（必背）

| 原级 | 比较级 | 最高级 |
|------|--------|--------|
| good / well | better | best |
| bad / badly | worse | worst |
| many / much | more | most |
| little | less | least |
| far | farther / further | farthest / furthest |

### 3.3 常用句型

| 句型 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| A + be + 比较级 + than + B | Tom is taller than Jack. |
| A + be + the + 最高级 + in/of | She is the tallest in her class. |
| as + 原级 + as | I am as tall as my brother. |
| not as/so + 原级 + as | He is not as tall as his father. |

## 五、介词 (Prepositions)

### 5.1 时间介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| at | 具体时刻 | at 7 o'clock, at noon, at night |
| on | 具体某天/日期 | on Monday, on May 1st, on my birthday |
| in | 月份/年份/季节/早中晚 | in May, in 2024, in summer, in the morning |
| for | 持续一段时间 | for two hours, for a week |
| since | 从某时开始（常与完成时连用） | since Monday, since 2020 |
| before | 在...之前 | before lunch, before 9 o'clock |
| after | 在...之后 | after school, after dinner |
| during | 在...期间 | during the holiday, during class |

### 5.2 地点介词

| 介词 | 用法 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| in | 在...里面 | in the box, in the room, in London |
| on | 在...上面（接触） | on the table, on the wall, on the floor |
| at | 在某地点 | at home, at school, at the bus stop |
| under | 在...下面 | under the bed, under the tree |
| behind | 在...后面 | behind the door, behind you |
| in front of | 在...前面 | in front of the house |
| next to | 在...旁边 | next to the bank, next to me |
| between | 在两者之间 | between the chairs |
| above | 在...上方（不接触） | above the clouds |
| below | 在...下方 | below the surface |

## 六、连词 (Conjunctions)

| 连词 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| and | 和、并且 | I like apples and oranges. |
| but | 但是 | I'm tired but I can't sleep. |
| or | 或者 | Do you want tea or coffee? |

| because | 因为 | I stayed home because I was sick. |

| if | 如果 | If it rains, we'll stay home. |
| although | 虽然 | Although it was cold, he went swimming. |
| before | 在...之前 | Wash your hands before you eat. |
| after | 在...之后 | I'll call you after I finish work. |

## 八、There be 句型

**用法：** 表示「某处有某物」，be 动词根据最近的名词决定单复数（就近原则）

| 时态 | 单数/不可数 | 复数 |
|------|-------------|------|
| 现在 | There is a book on the desk. | There are two books. |
| 过去 | There was a cat in the garden. | There were many people. |
| 将来 | There will be a party. | There will be many guests. |
| 否定 | There isn't / wasn't any milk. | There aren't / weren't any chairs. |

---

## 九、被动语态 (Passive Voice) - 基础

**结构：** be + 过去分词 (by + 动作执行者)

| 时态 | 主动语态 | 被动语态 |
|------|----------|----------|
| 一般现在时 | People speak English. | English is spoken. |
| 一般过去时 | Tom built the house. | The house was built by Tom. |

## 十、冠词 (Articles)

| 冠词 | 用法 | 例子 |
|------|------|------|
| a | 泛指单数可数名词（辅音开头） | a book, a dog, a university |
| an | 泛指单数可数名词（元音开头） | an apple, an hour, an umbrella |
| the | 特指/独一无二/序数词前 | the sun, the first, the book you bought |
| 零冠词 | 不可数名词泛指/复数泛指/专有名词 | I like music. Dogs are friendly. |
